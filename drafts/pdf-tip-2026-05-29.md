Topic: Add alternative text to every image, chart, and shape in Word

In Word, right-click any picture, chart, icon, SmartArt graphic, or shape and choose View Alt Text to open the Alt Text pane. Write a short, specific description of what the visual conveys in context — what the reader would lose if the image were missing — and avoid filler like "image of" or "picture showing." For a chart, describe the trend or the takeaway, not just the chart type. If the visual is purely decorative (a divider line, a background flourish), tick "Mark as decorative" so screen readers skip it. When the document is exported via Save As PDF, Word carries these descriptions through to the Figure tags' /Alt entries and marks decorative items as artifacts, which means the PDF arrives with image accessibility already in place and you do not have to retype alt text image by image in Acrobat's Tags panel later.

Reference: for the exact normative wording, see PDF/UA clause 7.3 in your ISO 14289-1 copy.
