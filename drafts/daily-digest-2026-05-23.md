**Both federal digital-accessibility deadlines governing the public sector have now been pushed back, but neither extension lightens the legal exposure facing covered organizations. In April, the Department of Justice gave state and local governments until April 2027 (entities serving 50,000+) and April 2028 (smaller entities and special districts) to bring websites and mobile apps to WCAG 2.1 Level AA under ADA Title II; weeks later, HHS extended its parallel Section 504 deadlines for federally funded healthcare and education recipients to May 2027 (15+ employees) and May 2028 (under 15). Disability-rights organizations have condemned both delays as rewarding inaction, and the key point bears repeating: an extended compliance date moves the clock, not the underlying duty. Title II's nondiscrimination obligations and Section 504's protections — on the books since 1973 — remain fully enforceable, and private plaintiffs can still sue today; website-accessibility filings actually rose in 2025, with governments, schools, universities, and healthcare providers a growing share of the targets. For any public entity or HHS-funded organization, the smartest use of the extra time is to keep remediating now rather than treat the new date as a reprieve.**
