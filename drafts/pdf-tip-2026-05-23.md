Topic: Tagging real content and logical reading order

A screen reader narrates a PDF in the order of its tag tree, not the order things appear visually on the page — so a document that looks perfect can still read back as gibberish. After tagging, walk the structure tree from top to bottom and confirm it follows the order a person would actually read: heading, then the body that belongs to it, then the next section. Multi-column layouts, sidebars, pull quotes, and captions are the usual troublemakers, because tools often tag content in the order they happened to find it rather than the order it should be read. While you're there, make sure only genuine content is in the tree at all: page numbers, decorative rules, and background graphics should be marked as artifacts so they drop out of the reading flow instead of interrupting it.

Reference: for the exact normative wording, see PDF/UA clause 7.1 in your ISO 14289-1 copy.
