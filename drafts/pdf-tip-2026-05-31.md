Topic: Headings and heading structure

In the tagged PDF, every heading should be a real heading tag (H1, H2, H3, and so on), and the levels should nest in order without skipping — H1 to H2 to H3, not H1 jumping straight to H4. Screen reader users navigate long documents by jumping heading to heading, so the heading outline is their table of contents. When remediating in Acrobat, open the Tags panel, check each visually-large piece of text, and retag any that came through as a P (paragraph) styled to look big. Two common fixes: promoting a page or section title that was tagged as P to H1, and demoting an H1 that was really a subheading so the outline does not have a flat run of same-level headings. Avoid using headings just to make text large — if a line is not actually starting a new section, it should be a P, not a heading.

Reference: for the exact normative wording, see PDF/UA clause 7.4 in your ISO 14289-1 copy.
