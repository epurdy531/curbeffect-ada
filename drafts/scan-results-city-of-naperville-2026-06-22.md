**7 of 8 default agenda PDFs are UNTAGGED** (no structure tree — unreadable to screen readers).

**Honest caveat (don't overstate this one):** Naperville is partly ahead of peers. In the current calendar view, 11 of 12 meetings ALSO publish an "Accessible Agenda" (a separate `M=AADA` link), and all 12 offer an HTML meeting-detail view. So an accessible version usually EXISTS — but the **primary "Agenda" link everyone clicks is the untagged PDF**, with accessibility relegated to a secondary link. That's a "separate-but-unequal" Title II angle, not "no alternative exists."
