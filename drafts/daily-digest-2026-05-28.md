**The National Federation of the Blind has sued DOJ and HHS in federal court in Maryland, arguing the agencies unlawfully pushed back the ADA Title II and Section 504 web-accessibility compliance deadlines without notice-and-comment rulemaking and asking a judge to vacate both Interim Final Rules. The underlying nondiscrimination duties are decades old, so whether or not the court restores the original 2026 dates, state and local governments, public schools and universities, and HHS-funded healthcare providers can be sued today — the safest read of this lawsuit is a reminder to keep remediating, not wait.**
