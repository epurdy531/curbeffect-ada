**Both federal digital-accessibility mandates for the public sector — DOJ's ADA Title II rule and HHS's Section 504 rule — now carry compliance dates pushed roughly a year later, but the extensions move the clock, not the duty. Title II and Section 504 have been enforceable for decades and federal website-accessibility filings rose in 2025, so state and local governments, public schools and universities, and HHS-funded healthcare providers can still be sued today; the smartest use of the extra time is to keep remediating, not to wait. Both rules are open for public comment (DOJ through June 22, HHS through July 6), with disability-rights groups mobilizing to oppose the delays.**
