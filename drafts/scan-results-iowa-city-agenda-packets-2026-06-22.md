PDF/UA-1 accessibility scan of 6 **agenda packets** from the City of Iowa City, IA City Council, pulled 2026-06-22 from their Granicus MediaManager portal (icgov.granicus.com, embedded on https://www.icgov.org/government/city-council/agendas-and-minutes). **Headline: every agenda packet scanned is UNTAGGED — zero structure tree, completely inaccessible to screen readers.** Iowa City runs Granicus, but Granicus publishes these packets as-is; it does not remediate the PDFs. Hundreds of pages of council business (ordinances, staff memos, correspondence) are unreadable to blind residents.

Lead with continued **litigation risk** — DOJ moved the Title II deadlines to Apr 2027 / Apr 2028, but these are public-facing documents a plaintiff can sue over today.
