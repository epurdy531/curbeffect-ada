Do you know whether all of the PDFs and documents your organization posts online meet the accessibility standards now required under [ADA Title II](https://www.ada.gov/resources/2024-03-08-web-rule/)? Those standards apply to the documents themselves — alternate formats are only an acceptable substitute under narrow technical or legal exceptions.

Most vendors aren't meeting that bar:

1. HTML transcripts — a separate web page, not an accessible PDF

3. Scan-and-audit tools — they flag problems but don't fix them
4. Accessibility overlays — a layer of code on top that doesn't fix the underlying document
5. Automated and AI auto-tagging — breaks down on complex layouts and government-specific formatting
