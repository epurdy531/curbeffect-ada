**Honest headline — it's MIXED, not a clean 'all inaccessible' pitch:**
- For council **Minutes**, Peoria publishes TWO copies of each: an **untagged "Adopted"** version (inaccessible) AND a separate **"Pdf" version that is fully PDF/UA-1 conformant** (0–3 failures). So accessible versions DO exist — Peoria is actively remediating.
- The **Agenda packets** are **tagged but still fail tens of thousands of checks** (49,953 and 61,422 failures) — a structure tree exists but it's far from conformant; manual review would surface real barriers.
