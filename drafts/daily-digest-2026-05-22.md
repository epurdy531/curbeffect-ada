The landscape is still defined by the one-year compliance extensions issued this spring — the DOJ's Title II web rule for state and local governments and the parallel HHS Section 504 extension for federally funded healthcare and education recipients. It is worth repeating that these extensions move the deadlines, not the liability: private plaintiffs and advocacy groups can still sue over inaccessible websites, documents, and apps today, and 2025 closed with a record number of web-accessibility lawsuits. For public agencies, schools, universities, and HHS-funded organizations, the extra year is best spent remediating now rather than waiting out the new dates.
